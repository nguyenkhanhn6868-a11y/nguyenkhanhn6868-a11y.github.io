CAREO Video Poster

CAREO Video Poster is an internal publishing tool used by CAREO, a used electric-car dealership in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. It uploads short car-review videos that our own sales team films and edits to our own TikTok account, with a caption and hashtags prepared in advance.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok and approves the requested permissions.
  2. The tool uploads an edited video file from our computer to TikTok using the TikTok Content Posting API.
  3. The caption, privacy setting and interaction settings are chosen by the account owner before each post.

The tool only posts to the account of the person who signed in. It does not read, store or share other users' data.
