Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how CAREO Video Poster ("the tool"), operated by CAREO, handles information when it is connected to a TikTok account.

Information we access

When the account owner signs in with TikTok, the tool receives an access token and basic account information (such as the display name and avatar) for the signed-in account, as permitted by the scopes the owner approves.

How we use it

The access token is used only to upload videos and captions chosen by the account owner to that same TikTok account. We do not use the information for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer. We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. We do not collect data about other TikTok users.

Revoking access

The account owner can disconnect the tool at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services, and can delete the locally stored token.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to CAREO through our Facebook page "Khánh Vinfast lướt Hồ Chí Minh".
